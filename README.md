EZ Cam records your screen, camera and voice, lets you polish the result on a multi-track timeline, and exports it or uploads it straight to your YouTube channel. It's made for tutorials, demos, lessons and walkthroughs, where you want a clean result without a steep learning curve.

---

## Download and install

1. Go to the **[latest release](https://github.com/StudioOneScripts/ezcam-releases/releases/latest)** and, under **Assets**, download `EZ-Cam-Setup-<version>.exe`.
2. Run it. EZ Cam installs for your user account (no admin rights needed) and adds a Start menu and desktop shortcut.
3. **Windows SmartScreen** may say "Windows protected your PC", because the installer isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Updates are automatic.** When a new version is out, EZ Cam shows a banner at the top of the window; click it to download, then restart to install. You can also use **Help → Check for Updates…**. Every version's changes are listed on the [releases page](https://github.com/StudioOneScripts/ezcam-releases/releases).

**Requirements:** Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

## What it does

### Recording
- Record a **whole screen, a single window or a region** you draw.
- Add your **webcam**, any number of **microphones**, **system sound**, and **ASIO** audio interfaces. Each source lands on its own track, so you can edit them separately.
- **Cursor recorded separately**, so afterwards you can make it bigger, change its colour, hide it, or add a glow, ring or spotlight that follows it.
- **Keystroke capture**: the shortcuts you press become on-screen keystroke callouts automatically. Ordinary typing is only recorded if you ask for it.

### Editing on the timeline
- Unlimited tracks. Move, trim, split, duplicate, copy and paste clips, alone or as a multi-selection.
- **Groups** keep clips on different tracks together.
- **In/Out ranges** and **ripple delete** to cut out mistakes and close the gaps across every track at once. Alt+drag moves a range as a whole, and looped playback repeats it.
- **Markers** on the ruler, which become **YouTube chapters** when you upload.
- **Right-click menus** for the common edits on clips, tracks, keyframes, markers and media, including **Remove empty tracks**.
- Change settings for **several clips at once**: audio, picture, cursor, transitions, shadow and text style.
- **Extend frame** (freeze) to hold a moment while you talk over it.
- **Clip speed** to speed up or slow down a section.
- **Close zoom** down to single milliseconds, with a detailed waveform, for precise audio edits.
- Snap, undo/redo for nearly everything, autosave and crash recovery.

### Annotations
- Titles, callouts, arrows, shapes, highlights, **spotlight** and **blur** (to hide private details), and images such as logos.
- Full text styling: font, colours, box, outline and shadow.

### Animation
- **Keyframe animation** of position, size, rotation and opacity for any clip, with easing (smooth, overshoot, bounce, elastic and more).
- **Animate mode**: move the playhead, drag or resize the clip on the preview, and a keyframe is made; drag an arrow's end to turn it. A dotted motion path shows where it goes, and keyframes can be dragged on the timeline to retime them.
- Ready-made **animations** (slide in, pop, Ken Burns, picture-in-picture…) and **behaviors** for annotations (typewriter, fly in, slide, pulse, float…).
- **Zoom-n-Pan**, including **automatic zooms** that follow your clicks and typing.
- **Transitions** between clips: cross-dissolve, push, slide, wipe, dip to black and more.
- **3D transitions**: cube rotate, flip, doors open and swing in, drawn in true perspective.
- Change a transition's length by **dragging its edge** on the timeline. Between two clips, both halves change together so it stays centred on the cut.

### Visual effects
- Shadow, rounded corners, colour adjustments, blur and other effects.
- **Chroma key** (green screen) for your camera, with an eyedropper to pick the colour.

### Audio
- Volume, fades and **volume envelopes** you draw right on the clip.
- **Voice clean-up** tools that work on a copy, never your original file:
  - **Noise removal** for hiss, fan noise and hum
  - **De-click** for mouth clicks, lip smacks and crackle
  - **De-plosive** for the thump of "p" and "b" sounds, only while it happens
  - **De-breath** to turn down breaths between phrases
  - **De-ess** to soften harsh "s" and "sh" sounds
- Each tool has Light, Medium and Strong settings, plus **Fine-tune** sliders for custom settings, and **Hear only what's removed** to check that only the noise is going.
- Voice **EQ** and a **compressor** that only turns loud parts down (no hidden boost).
- **Loudness normalizing**, and **automatic ducking** of music under your voice, with an adjustable fade back up.
- Per-track volume with level meters, plus a master **level meter with LUFS** to keep your mix at YouTube loudness.
- A **master limiter** with input gain and ceiling, so loud moments never distort. Playback and export sound exactly the same.
- Choose the **playback device**, such as your audio interface or headphones, in Settings.
- **Narration** recording straight onto the timeline, and **text to speech** (including ElevenLabs voices).

### Reuse
- **Library**: save intros, outros, lower thirds, logos and music once and drop them into any project. Saved designs scale to fit any canvas size, including vertical video.
- **Starter designs** come with the app: 8 lower thirds and 10 titles, most of them animated (news bar, social handle, typewriter, cinematic, neon, subscribe button and more), all fully editable.
- **Presets**: save a clip's look, audio settings or animation and apply it to other clips with one click.

### Export and share
- Fast **MP4** export, plus **WebM** and **GIF**, at the resolution and frame rate you choose.
- **Upload to YouTube** from inside the app: title, description, privacy, chapters from your markers, and a custom thumbnail, which you can grab from a frame of the video.

## Getting help

EZ Cam has a full illustrated user guide built in: press **F1** or choose **Help → User Guide**.

Your recordings and projects stay on your computer. EZ Cam only goes online to check GitHub for updates, to upload to YouTube when you choose to, and to ElevenLabs if you use text to speech with your own ElevenLabs key. See the [privacy policy](https://studioonescripts.github.io/ezcam-site/privacy.html).
